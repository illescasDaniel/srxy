1 · GUI — Filters dialog: “Which files to scan” moves here, ignore list first

The whole “Which files to scan” section (subfolders, archives, noisy files) moves from Search options into Filters, as the first section, with the new ignore list as its first item. Limits and Sensitivity follow below in the same scroll area.

Review only — “Add file… / Add folder…” opens the native chooser; a pick inside the search root is appended as an anchored pattern (/src/old/), outside the root as an absolute path (/home/me/x/). Duplicates are not added twice.
Review only — Search options dialog after the move: it keeps “Where to search” and “How to match” only; the “Which files to scan” section, its “Noisy files” subsection and the binary hint are removed from it (GUI and TUI). The six switches keep their current meaning and CLI flags; they are only edited in Filters now. Persistence (approved): the switches are stored under Filters and saved only when “Remember filters” is ticked; settings from 1.7 (switches inside the saved options) are migrated on load. the Filters “Reset to defaults” resets them, the Options “Reset” no longer touches them. OK in Filters applies filters and these switches together; Cancel discards both.
